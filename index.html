Welcome to the Urus Encyclopedia

From the historical archives of Urus

The Urus Encyclopedia is a comprehensive collection of historical, geographical, magical, and biological information concerning the world of Urus.

This encyclopedia contains information compiled from surviving historical records, scholarly research, governmental archives, and other documented sources.

Due to the loss and destruction of numerous records throughout Urus' history, some information remains incomplete or disputed.

Featured Article

The Great War

The Great War, also known as the War of the Gods, was a global conflict that lasted thirteen generations and fundamentally altered the political, geographical, and magical landscape of modern Urus.

The conflict resulted in unprecedented destruction, the deaths of an estimated 60% of the human population and 80% of the godly population, and the extinction of at least 42 species.
