Species Affected by the Great War

The Great War had a profound impact on the biodiversity of Urus. The destruction caused by the conflict affected both magical and non-magical species across large portions of the known world.

Surviving historical and biological records indicate that at least 23 species were rendered extinct during or as a direct consequence of the war. A further 42 species experienced severe population decline and remain significantly reduced compared to their pre-war populations.

The exact number of affected species may be considerably higher. Records concerning numerous species were lost during the conflict, while the classification of several populations remains disputed among modern researchers.

Reliability of Historical Records

Records concerning the populations and distribution of species prior to the Great War are incomplete. The destruction of archives, geographical changes, and the loss of biological records make precise population estimates difficult to establish.

As a result, classifications and population estimates may be revised when new historical evidence is discovered.
