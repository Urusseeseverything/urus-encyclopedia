Human Magical Aptitude

Introduction

Following the path of the Gods, human magic is divided into two primary alignments: Suhr and Sei. Most magic-wielding humans are Suhr-aligned and possess relatively mild magical capabilities. Unlike divine beings, a human cannot display magical competencies associated with both alignments.

Magical abilities may manifest at birth or during developmental milestones as an infant, with fewer cases being discovered later in life. The age and circumstances of manifestation vary considerably between individuals.

Mages will rarely reach high levels of attunement unless raised around magical creatures displaying similar forms of magic, taught at Magic Schools, or instructed by professionals in other settings. Such training may greatly improve a mage's abilities, but does not guarantee that they will unlock their full magical potential.

The number of humans capable of wielding magic represents a minority of the total human population. Estimates vary between regions and historical periods.

Attunement and Training

Magical attunement refers to an individual's ability to perceive, control, and effectively utilize their magical abilities. Natural aptitude varies considerably between individuals, and possessing magical abilities does not necessarily indicate a high level of attunement.

Exposure to magical creatures capable of displaying similar forms of magic may contribute to the development of a human mage's abilities. Formal education at Magic Schools is another established method of improving magical attunement.

Individuals may also receive instruction from trained professionals outside formal educational institutions. However, training does not guarantee that a mage will reach their theoretical maximum potential.
